App Permission Guide

Map Android permissions to user-facing reasons. Request only permissions required for active product features.

Camera

Use for taking photos, scanning documents, QR scanning, or video capture. Explain when capture starts and whether media leaves the device.

Location

Use for maps, nearby results, delivery, trip tracking, or geofencing. Prefer approximate location when precise location is not essential.

Notifications

Use for reminders, account activity, task alerts, and time-sensitive updates. Let users control categories where possible.

Storage and Photos

Use the system photo picker or scoped storage before requesting broad access. Clearly state whether files are uploaded or processed locally.

Microphone

Use for voice notes, audio calls, recording, or speech features. Show visible recording states and avoid background recording unless required and disclosed.

Drafts are templates, not legal advice. Review with qualified counsel before publishing.
